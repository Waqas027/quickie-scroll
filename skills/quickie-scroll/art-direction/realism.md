# Realism checklist — apply to every photoreal look (1–5)

*The seven clauses that separate "AI video" from a film. Apply every one; check for them again at review.*

**Read with:** [`art-direction/looks.md`](looks.md) · [`core/quality.md`](../core/quality.md) · [`video/prompting.md`](../video/prompting.md)

---

The gap between "AI video" and "a film" is almost entirely these seven clauses. Put them
in the prompts; check for them when reviewing a clip.

1. **Name a real lens and aperture.** "35mm at f/2.0" produces a defensible depth of
   field; "cinematic" alone produces a flat, over-sharp everything-in-focus frame.
2. **Name one light source and let the rest fall off.** Evenly-lit frames read as CG.
3. **Demand imperfection.** Grain, dust, fingerprints, worn edges, a slightly crooked
   object. Perfect surfaces are the strongest tell.
4. **Demand depth separation.** Foreground / midground / background must move at
   different rates — "foreground objects sliding past in parallax". A frame where
   everything moves together is a pan across a matte painting.
5. **Restrain the palette.** Two dominant values plus one accent. Colourful is the
   default AI failure mode.
6. **Never let the model write.** Every prompt ends with the no-text clause; any frame
   that renders letters is a re-roll, not a crop.
7. **Motion is slow.** Under-crank everything. Fast camera moves expose temporal
   artefacts; slow moves hide them and read as expensive.
